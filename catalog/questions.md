## Pattern notes

- `crown-shaper`: The prefix grades are the owner's: 'Good Prefix' is T1/T2 life and 'OK Prefix' is the Area of Effect support. Best-in-slot research for a Shaper Crown puts them the other way round - the item is a pseudo eight-link for the socketed gem, so the support-granting prefix is the one worth having and life is merely acceptable - and it counts the elevated Hypothermia (Level 25) as worth only 1-3% over Level 20, which is why the ladder's top class is Level 20 or better. Overrule the labels if the guides are right.

- `crown-shaper`: 'Any Crafted Prefix' also takes the bench '+1 to Level of Socketed AoE Gems' craft, which the guides call the standard finish for this item; if that craft is the intended one, the slot could name it instead of taking any crafted prefix.

- `crown-shaper`: The Hypothermia ladder is Level 16, 18, 20 and an elevated Level 25; there is no Level 22 (repoe 3.29.3.3, poedb, Craft of Exile all agree, and 3.30.0 announced nothing). The owner's 'Level 22 [elevated]' is most likely the Level 22 Blastchain Mine modifier, which shares the 'of Shaping' name. The elevated tier is best possible and Level 18 or better is good enough.

### `metamod.level-28`
